Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Mezhenon Production ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes mobile games and applications.

This Privacy Policy explains how our applications handle information.

1. Information We Collect

Our applications may store game progress and application settings locally on your device.

At this time, Mezhenon Production does not directly collect, store, or process personal information through our applications.

We do not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

2. Local Data

Some information may be stored locally on your device, including:

  • game progress;
  • in-game settings;
  • audio settings;
  • unlocked or upgraded game content.

This information is used only to provide application functionality and is not transmitted to Mezhenon Production.

3. Third-Party Services

Our applications may in the future use third-party services such as:

  • Google Play services;
  • advertising services;
  • analytics services;
  • crash reporting services.

If such services are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the information they may collect and how it is used.

Third-party services may operate under their own privacy policies.

4. Advertising

Our applications currently do not directly collect personal information for advertising purposes.

If advertising is added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect the relevant data practices.

5. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children through our applications.

6. Data Sharing

Mezhenon Production does not sell personal information.

We do not share personal information collected directly by our applications because our current applications do not directly collect such information.

7. Data Security

Game progress and settings stored locally on the device remain under the control of the user's device and operating system.

Users should be aware that uninstalling an application or clearing its application data may remove locally stored game progress.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our applications, services, or legal requirements change.

Changes will be published on this page and the effective date will be updated.

9. Applications Covered

This Privacy Policy applies to applications published by Mezhenon Production unless a specific application provides a separate privacy policy.
